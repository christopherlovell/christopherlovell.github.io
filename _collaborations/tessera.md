title: Tessera
description: Composite zoom simulation suites for emulation and inference
details: Tessera is a suite of thousands of dark-matter-only zoom resimulations, each with a different cosmology and large-scale environment, run with SWIFT and post-processed with the SHARK semi-analytic model and Synthesizer. Emulators trained on the suite predict halo and galaxy populations in gigaparsec volumes across a range of cosmological and astrophysical parameters. I lead the project from Cambridge.
